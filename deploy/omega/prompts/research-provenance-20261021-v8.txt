## Provenance discipline (NEW, MANDATORY — this is the point of this campaign)

Every NEW fact you store in DAS this campaign MUST follow this pipeline, in this order:

1. **Search:** use `websearch`. Results now include URLs: `(TITLE: ... URL: ... SNIPPET: ...)`. Pick sources worth reading.
2. **Archive at research time:** for each source you will actually use, run `metta` with `(archive-source "https://the-url")`. It fetches the page and commits it into the provenance git repo. Success returns `ARCHIVE-OK git:<40-hex-sha> sha256:<blob> path=<repo path> (...)`. That `git:<sha>` is THE provenance token for every fact derived from that source. If you get `ARCHIVE-FAILED: ...`, read the reason; you may use a different source, but a fact may NEVER cite a source that was not archived.
3. **Write the fact with provenance and epistemic status:** store facts via the extended add-atom form:

   `(metta "(add-atom &persistent (Inheritance (Concept \"Thing\") (Concept \"Category\")) \"https://the-source-url\" \"git:<sha-from-archive-source>\" (stv 0.9 0.8))")`

   - The URL and the `git:<sha>` must be the ones returned by YOUR `archive-source` call (they travel together).
   - `(stv <strength> <confidence>)` — both numbers in [0,1] — is your truth value for the fact. Use it honestly: well-corroborated primary-source facts ~ (0.9, 0.8); single secondary-source claims lower (e.g. (0.7, 0.5)); uncertain extrapolations lower still. Facts without a stv are stored without epistemic status — prefer always giving one.
   - Success looks like `BRIDGE-PERSIST-OK: verified=<n> handles=... provenance=<handle> source=archived stv=(0.9,0.8)`. That is the ONLY success. `verified` + `provenance` present = durably stored WITH provenance.
   - `BRIDGE-PERSIST-WRITE-FAILED: ...` means the fact is NOT stored. Read the reason, fix, retry.
4. **You may also derive** new facts by reasoning over retrieved facts (PLN-style). For a derived fact, cite the sources of the premises: archive-source is not needed again if the premise sources are already archived this campaign; reuse their git shas and set the stv to reflect the inference.

The v1 form `(add-atom &persistent (Fact ...))` still works but stores a fact WITHOUT provenance — use it only for genuinely source-free facts (e.g. pure definitions), and say so in the finding note.
