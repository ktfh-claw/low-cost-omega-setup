# Lighting Knowledge — Light Sources, Darkness, and Illumination

## Darkness

Darkness is the absence of light. When a room has no light sources (natural or artificial), it is dark. In a dark room:
- Nothing is visible to the human eye
- The room's surfaces exist but cannot be seen
- The only way to see anything is to introduce a light source

## Artificial Light Sources

Artificial light sources are devices that produce light. Common types include:
- **Light bulbs** — glass enclosures containing a filament or gas that produces light when electricity passes through
- **Lamps** — devices that hold one or more light bulbs and can be placed on surfaces or mounted

## Turning On a Light

When you "turn on a bulb/lamp" in a dark room:
1. Electricity flows to the light source
2. The light source produces illumination
3. The light spreads throughout the room's interior
4. The room's surfaces (walls, floor, ceiling, objects) become visible

## Illumination and Visibility

Light illuminates a space by reflecting off surfaces. When a light source is activated:
- Light travels from the source to all surfaces in the room
- Some light is reflected back to the observer's eyes
- The observer can then see the illuminated surfaces

## Key Lighting Facts

- A dark room becomes illuminated when a light source is activated
- The light source itself is also visible (you can see the bulb/lamp)
- Light does not pass through opaque surfaces (walls, ceiling, floor)
- In a windowless room, no external light enters; all visibility depends on artificial sources
- Turning on a light in a dark room reveals the room's interior, including the ceiling

## Relationship to the Windowless Room Prompt

The prompt states: "You turn on a bulb/lamp and look straight up."
- This establishes that a light source has been activated
- The room is now illuminated
- The observer is looking upward
- What they see above them is the ceiling (and possibly the light source itself, depending on its position)

## Summary

- Darkness = no light
- Turning on a light = introducing an artificial light source
- The light illuminates the room's interior surfaces
- Looking up in an illuminated room reveals the ceiling
- The ceiling is between the observer and the sky (it blocks the view)
