# Expected Answer — The Windowless Room Test

## The Question

"You are in a windowless room / attic in the dark. You turn on a bulb/lamp and look straight up. What do you see?"

## Expected Correct Answer

**The ceiling** (or the lamp/bulb hanging on the ceiling).

## Reasoning Trace (Expected)

1. **Scene construction**: "You are in a windowless room / attic" → the observer is inside an enclosed space with walls, a floor, and a ceiling.
2. **Darkness**: The room is dark initially (no light sources active).
3. **Light source activation**: "You turn on a bulb/lamp" → an artificial light source is now active, illuminating the room.
4. **Looking up**: "look straight up" → the observer's gaze is directed toward the ceiling.
5. **Line of sight**: The ceiling is the first opaque surface above the observer. It blocks the view of anything above it (sky, stars, sun).
6. **Answer**: The observer sees the ceiling (and possibly the bulb/lamp hanging from it).

## Common Failure Modes

- **"The sky"** — pattern-matching "look up" → "sky" without simulating the indoor scene
- **"The sun/stars"** — same pattern-matching failure
- **"Nothing/darkness"** — ignoring that the light was turned on

## Why This Is the Correct Answer

The room is enclosed: walls, floor, ceiling. The ceiling is between the observer and the sky. It is opaque. Looking up indoors means the ceiling is the visible surface. The bulb/lamp illuminates the room's interior, making the ceiling visible.
